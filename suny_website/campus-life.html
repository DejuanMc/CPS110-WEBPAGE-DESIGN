More Than a Campus

Life at SUNY New Paltz extends well beyond the classroom. Students join clubs, cheer on the Hawks, perform on stage, volunteer in the community, and explore the trails of the Shawangunk Ridge.

With a lively downtown just steps from campus, there's always something to do, somewhere to eat, and someone new to meet.
