Explore Your Possibilities

At SUNY New Paltz, students can explore a wide range of academic disciplines while developing the skills and knowledge needed to succeed beyond college.

Whether you're interested in the sciences, arts, education, business, technology, or the humanities, you can find opportunities to learn both inside and outside the classroom.
